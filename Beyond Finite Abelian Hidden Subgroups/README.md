# Beyond Finite Abelian Hidden Subgroups

Algorithms involving infinite abelian groups, nonabelian groups, and related extensions of hidden subgroup methods.

## Algorithm notebooks

- [Shor's Order-Finding and Factoring Algorithm](Shor%27s%20Order-Finding%20and%20Factoring%20Algorithm/README.md) uses phase estimation and continued fractions to recover an unknown multiplicative order, then applies classical gcd computations to factor a semiprime. Its exact hidden subgroup formulation is over the infinite group of integers.
